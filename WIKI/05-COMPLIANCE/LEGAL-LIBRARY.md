# Legal Library

## Purpose

Keep legal/regulatory research structured and reviewable.

## Source record

See `compliance/schema/legal-source.schema.json`.

Recommended fields:

- ID
- title
- jurisdiction
- category
- source type
- authority
- official URL
- verified date
- next review
- status
- project relevance
- actions
- notes

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:

1. official law/regulation
2. official authority guidance
3. official licensing/tariff source
4. court/official decision
5. reputable secondary analysis

## Versioning

Time-sensitive sources need a review date.

Do not silently keep old interpretations when a law or tariff changes.

## Full-text copies

Store full legal texts only when useful and lawful.

In many cases, an official link + relevant sections + project notes is easier to maintain than a stale local copy.
