# Public Transparency

A trustworthy platform can expose understandable information without exposing sensitive operations.

## Potential public areas

- privacy policy
- data rights
- cookies/device storage
- moderation rules
- reporting channel
- copyright/rights process
- AI transparency
- youth protection where applicable
- accessibility
- security contact
- service status
- transparency reports
- cancellation/withdrawal flows where applicable

## Separate public from internal evidence

Public transparency must not reveal:

- security architecture details useful to attackers
- personal case files
- internal investigation evidence
- secrets or credentials

## Version documents

Important public policies should have:

- version
- effective date
- change history
- stable identifier
