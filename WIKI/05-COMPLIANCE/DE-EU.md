# Germany / EU Compliance Pack

This pack is a **research and release-check structure**, not legal advice.

## Common areas to review

Depending on the product:

- GDPR / national data-protection law
- cookies/device storage
- digital-services/platform rules
- consumer contracts
- subscription cancellation/withdrawal
- accessibility
- youth protection
- copyright and content rights
- AI transparency
- taxation/invoicing
- cybersecurity obligations
- sector-specific rules

## Activation rule

Do not mark a law as "applicable" only because it exists.

For each source record:

- jurisdiction
- service type
- company/project situation
- applicability reasoning
- review date
- action
- unresolved questions

## Market expansion

A translated interface does not equal a legal market launch.

New countries may require new:

- consumer rules
- tax handling
- age/youth rules
- licenses
- public documents
- support language
