# Privacy by Design

## Core questions

For every processing activity:

- what data?
- whose data?
- why?
- legal/contractual basis where applicable?
- who can access it?
- how long?
- how is it deleted?
- can it be pseudonymized?
- does it need encryption?
- how can the person exercise rights?

## Privacy by default

New accounts/features should start with conservative visibility.

Examples:

- public activity off unless required
- marketing off without valid basis
- location not collected without need
- private history not visible to other users

## Separate identity from behavior

Analytics should use internal IDs instead of repeating email/name.

## Data minimization

Do not collect identity documents, phone numbers or location data merely because they might become useful later.

## Sensitive data

Use:

- field-level encryption where appropriate
- strict capabilities
- audit
- minimal retention
- limited export/logging

## Public transparency

Explain meaningful data practices in human-readable language, not only legal prose.
