# Data Subject Rights

Projects handling personal data should plan self-service and case-based workflows for applicable rights.

Typical areas may include:

- access
- correction
- deletion
- restriction
- portability
- objection
- consent withdrawal

The exact legal scope depends on jurisdiction and processing basis.

## Product model

A privacy center may show data categories, purpose, retention and available actions.

## Request tracking

Use a dedicated case model with:

- request ID
- type
- received time
- identity verification state
- due date
- assigned role
- status
- decision
- completion time

## Identity verification

Use the least intrusive verification that is sufficient.

Do not default to collecting identity documents.

## Exports

Privacy exports are themselves sensitive.

Protect them with:

- re-authentication
- short expiry
- non-guessable link/token
- audit
- automatic deletion
