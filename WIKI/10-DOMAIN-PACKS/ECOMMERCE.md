# E-Commerce Domain Pack

## Typical modules

- products
- variants
- prices
- carts
- orders
- payments
- refunds
- invoices
- entitlements
- support
- fraud controls

## Security

Never trust browser payment success alone.

Use verified server-side payment events/webhooks.

## Privacy

Avoid storing payment instrument data when a payment provider can hold it securely.

## Compliance

Consumer, tax, invoice, cancellation and accessibility requirements are market-dependent and require project-specific review.
