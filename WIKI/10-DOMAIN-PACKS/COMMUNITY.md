# Community Domain Pack

## Typical modules

- profiles
- relationships/follows
- posts/content
- reports
- moderation
- notifications
- blocking
- support
- trust/safety
- search

## Key risks

- harassment
- spam
- impersonation
- illegal content
- privacy leaks
- moderation abuse

## Design priorities

- clear visibility controls
- block/report tools
- moderation history
- appeal process
- role separation
- rate limits
