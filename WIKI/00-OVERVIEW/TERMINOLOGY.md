# Terminology

## Foundation

The reusable project-neutral rules, templates, schemas and guidance in this repository.

## Project Profile

The adopting project's `MGD_PLATFORM.yml`.

## Core

Capabilities that should not be casually removed, such as auth, permissions, security, audit and privacy controls.

## Module

A bounded feature area with explicit dependencies, permissions, events, data ownership and documentation.

## Domain Pack

A set of recommended modules, entities and checks for a broad project category.

## Backoffice

The internal administration shell used by admins, moderators, support or other privileged roles.

## Role

Human-readable grouping of permissions.

## Capability / Permission

A concrete action such as `support.case.read` or `content.publish`.

## Policy

Server-side decision logic determining whether an actor may perform an action on a resource.

## Service Principal

A non-human identity used by automation or AI agents.

## Entitlement

A right to use a module, feature, product or content item.

## Audit Event

A durable record of a privileged or business-relevant action.

## Security Event

A signal that may indicate misuse, attack or abnormal security behavior.

## Release Gate

A condition that must be satisfied before a feature/environment can be released.

## Legal Library

A structured source registry for laws, regulations, authority guidance and internal interpretation notes.
